Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, select Lexmia Pro and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal. You keep Pro until the period ends.

How do I restore my purchase?

Sign in to the App Store with the Apple ID you used to subscribe, then in Lexmia open Profile and tap Restore purchases (also available on the Pro screen).

How do I change the language?

Open Profile and choose Türkçe or English under Language. The app and its content switch immediately.

Does Lexmia work offline?

Yes. The term database is on your device, so search and browsing work without an internet connection.

I found an error in a term.

Use Profile > Send Feedback and choose Content error, or email us.
