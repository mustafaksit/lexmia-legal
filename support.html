Frequently asked questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, select Lexmia Pro and tap Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid renewal. You keep Pro until the period ends.

How do I restore my purchase?

Sign in to the App Store with the Apple ID you used to subscribe, then in Lexmia open Profile and tap Restore purchases (also available on the Pro screen).

How do I change the language?

Open Profile and choose Türkçe or English under Language. The app and its content switch immediately.

Does Lexmia work offline?

Yes. The term database is on your device, so search and browsing work without an internet connection.

I found an error in a term.

Use Profile > Send Feedback and choose Content error, or email us.

Sources and methodology

Lexmia is a medical terminology dictionary and vocabulary study tool that explains what terms mean. It does not diagnose, does not recommend treatments or doses, and does not give medical advice. This page explains where Lexmia's content comes from and how it was put together.

How the content was made

  • The term list, English term names, synonyms, ICD-10 codes and Wikipedia links come from Wikidata.
  • English definitions were taken from English Wikipedia summaries and Wikidata descriptions; more than half of them were later rewritten or corrected with AI.
  • All Turkish term names and everyday names were generated with AI (a large language model). For some terms that have a Turkish Wikipedia article, the Turkish definition was taken from that article; for the others it was written by AI-assisted translation from the English source text. As a result, most Turkish definitions were written with AI.
  • Almost all example sentences were written with AI.
  • Quiz questions were generated automatically from the definitions using templates; almost all answer options were rewritten with AI, and the English options and explanations were translated with AI.
  • Categories and difficulty levels were assigned by rules; some categories were corrected following AI suggestions.
  • The content went through several rounds of automated (AI-assisted) consistency and accuracy checks, and the errors found were corrected. In addition, where a reference text was available, each visible term's English and Turkish short definition was automatically (AI-assisted) compared with the term's own reference text (MeSH scope note, MedlinePlus summary, or Wikipedia/Wikidata text), looking only for contradictions; contradicting definitions were rewritten from the reference or the term was hidden. The developer corrected a limited number of terms by hand. The content has not been reviewed by a health professional.
  • Sentences giving patients dose or treatment instructions were reduced to plain definitions; Lexmia does not suggest diagnoses, treatments or doses.

Medical references for each term

Most term pages list, directly under the short definition, links to institutional pages about the same concept: MedlinePlus and MeSH (U.S. National Library of Medicine), ICD-10 (World Health Organization), ATC (WHO Collaborating Centre) and PubChem (NCBI) for drugs, and TA98 (FIPAT) for anatomy. These identifiers come from Wikidata and were checked automatically. These pages are not the source of Lexmia's text; they are trusted references you can use to check the concept.

ICD-10

ICD-10 codes come from Wikidata and were checked against the World Health Organization's ICD-10 (2019) list; codes not on the list or conflicting with Wikidata were corrected or removed.
