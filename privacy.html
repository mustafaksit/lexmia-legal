Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Summary

Lexmia is an offline medical dictionary. There is no account or sign-in. The term database, your favorites, search history and settings are stored only on your device and are never sent to us.

Data stored on your device

  • The medical term database bundled with the app.
  • Favorites, recently viewed terms, search count, daily streak, language and theme preferences.

This data stays on your device. Deleting the app removes it.

Feedback form

If you choose to send feedback, the form sends the following to us through the Web3Forms service, which delivers it to our email inbox:

  • The name, email address, subject and message you enter.
  • Technical details to help us fix problems: app version and build number, iOS version, device model, app language and theme preference.

Information sent with the Contact us form is delivered by Web3Forms and kept for at most 30 days; Web3Forms also records your IP address when you send it.

We use this only to reply to you and improve the app. If the form cannot be sent, the app can open your email app instead; in that case the message is sent by your email provider.

Purchases

Lexmia Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. To check whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives purchase information from Apple (such as product, purchase date and expiry) together with an anonymous user ID generated by the app. RevenueCat processes purchase data for subscription management and revenue reporting. We do not receive or store your payment details. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no usage analytics: we do not record how you use the app. Purchase data is used only as described under Purchases.
  • We do not sell your data. We share it only with the service providers named in this policy (Web3Forms, RevenueCat and Apple), for the purposes described here.

Children

Lexmia is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their personal data.

Your rights and contact

To ask about, correct or delete feedback you sent us, email mustafaaksit@outlook.com. We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
